# L1 Support call quality guidelines (sample)

These are the behaviours a Level 1 support agent is expected to show on every
inbound call. Each section carries a weight; the weights total 100.

## 1. Greeting and identification (10)
Answer within the standard, state the company name and your own name, and offer
help. A met call has all three in the opening line.

## 2. Caller verification (10) — critical
Confirm the caller is who they say they are before discussing the account:
business name plus account number, PIN, or a listed authorised contact.
Skipping this entirely fails the call regardless of the rest.

## 3. Issue discovery and active listening (15)
Restate the problem in your own words, confirm the scope (one device, one
site, everything), and ask what changed. The caller should hear that you
understood.

## 4. Troubleshooting (20)
Follow a logical path from simplest to more involved, explain why each step is
being asked, and confirm the result of each step before moving on.

## 5. Communication (10)
Plain language, no unexplained jargon, one instruction at a time, and tell the
caller what to expect and how long things will take. Avoid dead air.

## 6. Empathy and professionalism (10)
Acknowledge the impact on the caller, stay calm, use their name, never blame.

## 7. Resolution or escalation (15)
Fix it on the call and prove it (a test call, a status check), or escalate
properly with a ticket number, an owner and a time the caller will hear back.

## 8. Closing (10)
Recap what was done, ask if there is anything else, give the reference number,
thank the caller.
